2 Problem Statement
The Research Intelligence Crisis at Scale
Samsung Research Institute faces a dual research intelligence failure that compounds silently every
week:
FAILURE 1
The Opportunity Gap
Engineers track hundreds of papers but lack a
system to automatically detect whether a new
paper materially affects an active project or fixes an
open issue. Senior engineers must make these
judgments manually — it never scales. High-value
papers sit in reading lists for months.
FAILURE 2
The Competitive Blind Spot
When Google Brain, Qualcomm AI, or Meta AI
publishes work overlapping Samsungʼs unpublished
R&D — the IP advantage is lost, patent novelty is
threatened, and project directions may need to
pivot. Today this is discovered by accident, weeks
after publication.
Root Cause: No autonomous agent maintains a living memory of internal engineering work and watches
the global research world to surface what matters to you specifically. RADAR solves both — one agent,
two intelligent modes, complete research situational awareness.

stale README.

Relevance
When a paper matches a repo, RADAR traces which

UPGRADE 03
Patent Proximity Scoring

team will build this.
